SENTIRE REMINDERS — ONE-TIME SETUP (about 10 minutes)
=====================================================
Your reminders live in YOUR Google account: a Google Sheet + 3 Google Calendars
(Sentire – ODS (Monthly), Sentire – Annual, Sentire – Business).
Your phone alerts you through the Google Calendar app, even when the page is closed.

PART 1 — Google script (on the laptop, once)
--------------------------------------------
1. Open https://script.google.com  →  New project.
2. Click "Untitled project" at the top and name it:  Sentire Reminders
3. Delete everything in the editor. Open reminders_apps_script.gs (in this folder) in Notepad,
   copy ALL of it, paste it into the editor, and press Ctrl+S.
4. Click ⚙ Project Settings (left side) → Time zone → "(GMT+05:30) India Standard Time".
5. Back in the editor (< > icon), choose the function  setup  in the drop-down at the top → click ▶ Run.
   Google asks for permission → choose your account → "Advanced" → "Go to Sentire Reminders (unsafe)"
   → Allow.  (It is your own script; Google shows this for every personal script.)
6. Open "Execution log" at the bottom. Copy the line  SECRET KEY: xxxxxxxx  — you need it in step 9.
7. Click Deploy (top right) → New deployment → ⚙ Select type → Web app.
     Description: Reminders
     Execute as:  Me
     Who has access:  Anyone
   → Deploy → copy the Web app URL (ends with /exec).
   ("Anyone" lets the page reach it; without the secret key nobody can read or change anything.)

PART 2 — Connect the Reminders page
-----------------------------------
8. Main Page → Reminders → tap "Local only · Connect" at the top.
9. Paste the Web app URL and the SECRET KEY → Connect.
   Reminders you already added on this device are uploaded to Google.

PART 3 — On your phone
----------------------
10. The page must be on an https address for the microphone and Share to work on a phone.
    Put these 6 files in your GitHub repository (drolia-daughters/order-form):
      reminders.html, reminders_agent.js, reminders_sw.js, reminders.webmanifest,
      reminders-192.png, reminders-512.png
    (GitHub → repository → Add file → Upload files → Commit.)
    Then open on the phone in Chrome:  https://drolia-daughters.github.io/order-form/reminders.html
11. Tap "Connect" and paste the same URL + key (once per phone).
12. Chrome menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app"). Allow the microphone when asked.
13. Install the Google Calendar app (if not already) and make sure the 3 "Sentire –" calendars
    are switched ON with notifications.

HOW TO USE
----------
• Tap 🎤 and say:  "remind me on 20th of every month to pay GST"           → ODS
                    "every year on 15 March renew factory licence"         → Annual
                    "tomorrow at 11 am meeting with bank"                  → Business
                    add "2 days before" for an early alert.
  Check the card, tap ✓ Save. Use the ODS / Annual / Business buttons on the card to move it.
• WhatsApp: open the message → long-press → Share → choose "Reminders" (after step 12),
  or copy it into the "Paste a WhatsApp message" box. The agent reads the date and title.
• Gmail: every morning at 7 AM the script looks for due dates, renewals, EMIs and premiums in
  the last 3 days of mail and puts them in "To review". Tap ✓ Add or ✕. Button: "Check Gmail now".
• Every morning at 8 AM you get an email with what is due today and in the next 7 days.
• ✓ Done on a monthly/annual reminder marks this time done; the next one stays.

LIMITS
------
• WhatsApp cannot be read automatically by any app — use Share or paste.
• Monthly reminders on the 29th–31st skip months that do not have that day.
• If you change the script later: Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → Version: New version → Deploy
  (the URL stays the same).
