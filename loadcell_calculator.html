The formulas (per load cell)
Step 1 Capacity at target (kg) = Capacity(t) × 1000 ÷ (Sensitivity ÷ Target)
Step 2 New weight (kg) = Step 1 + Allowance
Step 3 New sensitivity (mV/V) = Target × Capacity(t) × 1000 ÷ New weight
Step 4 Resistor total (Ω) = Input R × (New sensitivity ÷ Target − 1)  → “Cannot trim” if New sensitivity is below Target
Step 5 Per leg = Step 4 ÷ 2, rounded to the step above  → fit on Ex+ and Ex−
Step 6 Input R after resistors = Input R + 2 × per leg
Step 7 Shunt = Target input R × Step 6 ÷ (Step 6 − Target input R), rounded  → fit across Ex+/Ex−, outside the series resistors (does not change sensitivity)
Check: sensitivity with rounded resistors = New sensitivity × Input R ÷ (Input R + 2 × per leg)
