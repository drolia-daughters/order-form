📂 Migration

Save every document in its folder, then press 🔄 Migrate. The software reads each file (PDF or JPEG photo) and posts it: Sales and Purchase bills into Accounts, L.R. copies onto the despatched job, bank statements into Smart Finance, other documents filed under their party. If a document cannot be read, press 🎓 Teach once and type what it says — next time the same kind of document is read by itself.
